GZAPPLE is a music producer, composer, sound designer, and audio engineer who has been making music for over 9 years. Originally using FL Studio to create MIDI art, his curiosity quickly led him to explore the reaches of music production and discover just how much could be expressed through sound.

Rather than limiting himself to a single genre, he treats music as a form of self-expression. His work moves freely between classical, electronic, ambient, metal, cinematic, experimental, and everything in between, taking inspiration from each style while developing his own interpretation of it.

Outside of production, GZAPPLE spends his free time studying music theory, composition, film, and sound design. He enjoys understanding why music and sound behave the way they do, and using that knowledge to experiment, develop new ideas, and find new ways to express them.

His latest album, Return to Elysium, represents the culmination of this approach: a 15-song exploration of storytelling through different genres and styles, while paying homage to the artists and composers who came before him and creating something that may inspire those who come after.
